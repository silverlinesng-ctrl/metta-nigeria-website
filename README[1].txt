METTA SYSTEM LIMITED WEBSITE
=============================
Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js

How to use:
1. Open index.html in a browser to preview.
2. Upload all three files to your web host/public_html.
3. Point www.mettanigeria.com to the hosting account.
4. Replace the contact form email in index.html with your real business email.
5. Replace the text M logo with your official logo when available.

The design is responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile.
